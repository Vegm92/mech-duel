# Parts look

What each part should look like, from what it does in the game (Victor 2026-10-29: "each part should have its own design").
Every mech is the same dark **chassis** (frame, round joints) wearing **armor shells**. A part's shell tells you its job before you read its card.

## Rules for every part

- **Read from above.** The game camera looks down and a mech is ~60-100 px tall. The top silhouette carries the idea; small details are for close-ups and the garage.
- **Series = weight.** Light: slim, angular, more bare frame, fins. Standard: one clean shell per piece. Heavy: thick, rounded, stacked plates, big bolts.
- **Less cube.** Taper (wider at the top), rounder bevels, angled plates, a dark gap between shell and frame.
- **Colors.** `body` = team paint (the customization hook), `trim` = grey steel, `joint` = dark frame, `edge` = worn steel. Glow slots (`core`, `boost`, `glow`, `gem`) say the element.
- **Keep the fit.** Object names, pivots and attach points never move (see `CLAUDE.md`). Keep each part close to its current triangle count: shape, not detail.

## Heads (pivot at the neck, face = +X)

- **Rookie Helm** (starter, no E): a plain rounded helmet with a visor slit and ear bolts. The friendly default face.
- **Rhino Horn** (E: Lunge): a forward-leaning wedge helmet with one big curved horn on the nose. It should look like it charges head-first.
- **Stag Pincers** (E: Hook): a narrow head with two big mandible claws reaching forward, like a stag beetle, and a cable spool on the back for the hook.
- **Sun Crest** (light, E: Cleanse): a small light head with a fan of sun rays behind it like a halo and a glowing gem on the forehead.
- **Radar Dish** (E: X-Ray): a squat head with a big tilted radar dish on top and one wide scanner eye.
- **Sniper Visor** (lightest, E: Focus): a tiny slim head that is mostly one long scope lens pointing forward. Fragile and precise.
- **Medic Dome** (heavy, E: Repair Pulse): a big round dome on a thick collar with a glowing green cross. A sturdy ambulance light.
- **Ward Orb** (sturdy, E: Frost Field): a solid head holding a floating icy orb in a ring above it.
- **Tri-Antenna** (E: Electric Wall): a flat head with three tall antennas with glowing tips, like a power pylon.
- **Mine Hatch** (E: Trap): an armored box head with an open hatch on top and mines showing inside.
- **Oni Horns** (heaviest, E: Brace): a huge mask-like head with two big curved horns, an angry brow and a fanged jaw plate. The scariest head.

## Cores (chest add-on + both shoulder pads; glow color = the R skill)

- **Furnace** (runs hot, R: Lance): two smokestacks, glowing grille vents, heat-darkened pads. A hot boiler.
- **Coolant** (cools fast, R: Stasis): glass coolant tanks on the pads, pipes into the chest, frosty glow.
- **Surge Reactor** (R meter fast, R: Overdrive): an open glowing reactor ring in the chest with spark rods. Overcharged.
- **Leech** (drains life, R: Repair): a dark ribbed chest with a red pump like a heart and fang-like tubes on the pads.
- **Titan** (heavy, +armor, R: Mines): massive stacked plates and thick pads with mine racks. A fortress.
- **Volt** (light, dash, R: EMP): a slim chest with tesla coils on the pads and lightning-yellow stripes.
- **Fury** (more damage when hurt, R: Berserk): spiky pads with blades and an angry red eye in the chest.
- **Flywheel** (light, damage while moving, R: Swarm): a spinning disc in the chest and missile cells on the pads.
- **Aegis** (heavy, shield, R: Bubble): round shield-shaped pads and a blue dome emitter in the chest.
- **Glass** (light, +damage −armor, R: Orbital): a fragile faceted pink crystal, thin pads, a targeting antenna.
- **Phantom** (light, R: Blink): swept-back ghostly fins and hollow see-through ribs.
- **Gravity** (long hook, R: Gravity Well): a purple ring orbiting a dark sphere, heavy anchored pads.
- **Bastion** (heavy, big shield, R: Bastion wall): castle battlement pads and a wall-panel chest.
- **Meltdown** (hot = more damage, R: Meltdown): a cracked chest showing orange glow, warning stripes, vent pipes.
- **Deflector** (parry, R: Deflect): angled fins on the pads that look like they bend shots away.
- **Mirror** (reflect, R: Mirror): a bright polished mirror front plate and smooth chrome pads.
- **Rail** (fast bullets, R: Rail Shot): twin rail rods along the chest and long sleek pads. Sniper tech.
- **Shroud** (light, fast, R: Shroud smoke): a hooded cowl over the chest with smoke vents. Dark and stealthy.

## Arms (thin → thick; shoulder, elbow and fist never move)

- **Wire** (lightest, fastest hands): a bare skeleton arm of thin rods and cables with a tiny cuff.
- **Reed** (light): a slim segmented tube with dark rings, like bamboo.
- **Frame** (standard): one clean shell on the upper arm and forearm over a round elbow joint.
- **Brace** (extra plates): the frame arm plus a bolted plate on the outside and a wrist band.
- **Bulwark** (heavy): a chunky arm with a big flared guard on the outside of the forearm.
- **Anvil** (heaviest, slowest hands): a dark block forearm like an anvil, studs, a huge fist.

## Legs (they carry the dash move)

- **Thruster Greaves** (balanced, Dash): standard walker legs with shin plates and a thruster on each calf.
- **Hover Skirt** (fast, Glide): no legs, a flared skirt floating on a glowing ring.
- **Crawler Treads** (tank, Ram): two low, heavy tank tracks with a bumper in front.
- **Skitter Legs** (instant turns, Skitter hops): thin bug legs splayed wide, twitchy.
- **Twin Wheels** (top speed, Drift): two big wheels on short struts, sporty.
- **Jet Boots** (light, Leap): slim legs with jet pods and glowing soles, ready to jump.
- **Quake Stompers** (heaviest, Slam): huge thick legs with big toed feet.
- **Rail Skates** (fast, Double dash): inline skate boots on a rail.
- **Quad Strider** (steady, Pounce): four sturdy animal legs with paws, like a big cat.
- **Unicycle** (Roll): one big wheel in a fork.

## Boosters (on the back; dash distance + trail)

- **Flicker Jet** (short dash, arc trail): a slim pack, one nozzle, two spark prongs.
- **Wisp Fins** (quick recharge, frost trail): two swept fins with frosty glowing edges.
- **Back Thruster** (standard, flame trail): a dark pack with two nozzles.
- **Twin Thruster** (longer, smoke trail): a wide pack, two big nozzles, smoke stacks.
- **Comet Engine** (long heavy dash, magnet): one big round engine inside glowing coil rings.
- **Rocket Pack** (longest, no trail): two tall rockets with nose cones.

## Weapons (arm = replaces the forearm, shoulder = on the pad, else held)

- **Rivet Cannon** (arm, all-rounder): a short rugged barrel with a rivet feeder on top.
- **Needle Rifle** (held, slow and hard): a long thin crossbow rifle.
- **Scatter Gun** (arm, close range): a wide stubby cannon with a cluster of 5 holes.
- **Buzz Gatling** (arm, hold to fire): a spinning barrel cluster with an ammo drum.
- **Blaze Thrower** (shoulder, fire cone): a fuel tank with a nozzle and a pilot flame.
- **Hornet Pod** (shoulder, 2 homing missiles): a pod with two missile tips showing.
- **Thump Mortar** (shoulder, lobbed shell): a short fat tube angled up.
- **Prism Laser** (held, instant beam): a handgun with a crystal prism lens.
- **Arc Coil** (arm, zap): a coil with copper rings and a prong tip.
- **Bounce Rifle** (held, bouncing bolts): a rifle with an angled bumper muzzle.
- **Gyro Saw** (melee, quick wide slash): a big circular blade on a hub.
- **Drill Lance** (melee, long thrust): a long cone drill.
- **Shock Hammer** (melee, knockback): a big hammer head with shock coils.
- **Rake Claws** (melee, two fast slashes): a gauntlet with three long rake claws.
- **Chain Lash** (melee, longest reach, yank): chain links with a hook tip.
- **Piston Spike** (melee, short, huge damage): a gauntlet with a fat piston and a spike.
- **Ion Blade** (melee, fast energy cut): a sword with a glowing edge.
- **Riot Plate** (melee, shield bash): a big riot shield plate.
- **Rocket Fist** (melee, rockets forward): a big fist with a jet on its back.
- **Wrecking Flail** (melee, hits all around): a spiked ball on a chain.
