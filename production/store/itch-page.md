## Short description

Build a robot from five parts and two weapons. Beat a ladder of rival mechs. Win their parts.

## Description

Mech Duel is a top-down arena fighter. You aim every shot yourself: no auto-aim, no auto-attacks.

**Build your mech.** Head, core, arms, legs and booster, plus a weapon in each hand. 63 parts and 20 weapons: cannons, mortars, lasers, flamers, saws, hammers, hooks. Your core gives you an R skill, your head an E skill, your legs a dash.

**Fight the ladder.** 12 rival builds wait in the stadium. First to 3 rounds wins. Break an arm and its weapon hits softer and reloads slower; break the legs and they limp.

**Win their parts.** Every win lets you keep one part the rival lost. Beat them again and they come back stronger.

**Pick your challenge.** Easy, Normal or Hard: the rival aims, predicts, dodges and moves better as you go up.

**Watch the knockout.** The last K.O. of every round plays back as a slow-motion replay.

## Controls

| Move | W A S D | Left stick |
| Aim | Mouse | Right stick |
| Left / right weapon | Left / right click | LT / RT |
| Shield (hold, time it to parry) | Q | RB |
| Head skill | E | L3 |
| Dash | Space | LB |
| R skill | R | R3 |
| Pause | Esc | Start |
| Mute / music / language | M / N / L | |

## Install

Unzip, run `MechDuel.exe`. Windows may warn that the app is unknown: click **More info → Run anyway**. Saves are in `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Mech Duel`.
